# Chromosome Browser

The chromosome browser is a tool to visualize a person's DNA matches. It shows the DNA segments shared with other people in the tree as colored bars on the chromosomes.

You can find it under the &ldquo;Chromosome Browser&rdquo; tab in the DNA view.

For each chromosome, the upper (light blue) bar represents the copy inherited from the father, the lower (light red) bar the one inherited from the mother. The shared segments are shown as colored bars on top of these. Segments whose side (paternal or maternal) is not known are shown in a separate track between the two. Where segments of several matches overlap, they are drawn in separate, thinner lanes, so that every segment stays visible.

Clicking on the name of a match in the legend, only this person's shared segments are shown.

## Grouping by common ancestors

Above the legend, you can switch between coloring the segments by **Matches** and by **Common ancestors**. With common ancestors, matches who descend from the same most recent common ancestors with the selected person share a color and a row in the legend. This shows at a glance which parts of the chromosomes were inherited from which ancestral couple – a segment of another match that overlaps with those of a group on the same side probably comes from the same ancestors, which helps to place a new, unknown match.

The groups are ordered by the closest relationship first. Matches without known common ancestors are grouped under "Unknown". A match who is related through several lines is grouped by the closest one.

## Saving the chart

To save the chart, click the download button next to the person selector. It downloads an SVG file with a white background and the legend, including each match's total shared centimorgans. If a match or group is selected in the legend, only that one is included.
